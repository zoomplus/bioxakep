# CS2 Performance Optimization Guide for ADHD Players
## Headshot Consistency in Matchmaking

**Strengths:**
- Excellent performance in 1v1 duels
- Good response to L-theanine (200mg improved microcontrol)
- Solid supplement inventory and neurochemical understanding

**Problems in MM:**
- Body shots instead of headshots
- Impulsivity and A+D spam

## Optimized Supplement Protocols

### Training Sessions ("Drive" Mode)
**Timing and Dosing:**
- T-90: L-Tyrosine 1.0g (only if tired/underslept)
- T-60: Alpha-GPC 300-450mg
- T-45: Caffeine 135mg + L-Theanine 125-150mg
- T-30: Noopept 20mg (optional)
- T-15: Fast carbs 10-20g + electrolytes

**When to use:** Aim training, skill building, when you need maximum drive and can handle some impulsivity

### Matchmaking Sessions ("Control" Mode)
**Primary Stack:**
- T-60: Alpha-GPC 300mg
- T-45: Caffeine 135mg + L-Theanine 175-225mg
- T-30: Noopept 10mg (optional)

**L-Theanine Optimization for 135mg Caffeine:**
- **Base dose:** 175mg (1.3:1 ratio)
- **High stress/important match:** 200-225mg
- **Maximum per session:** 300mg
- **Never exceed:** 2.2:1 theanine:caffeine ratio

**Tyrosine Decision Matrix:**
- ✅ Use 500mg max if severely tired (T-90)
- ❌ Avoid if feeling normal energy
- ❌ Never use if already feeling "wired"
- ❌ Skip if previous session caused rushes

### Second Session (5-8 hours later)
- Caffeine 50-75mg + L-Theanine 100mg (T-30)
- No repeat of Alpha-GPC, Tyrosine, or Noopept

## MM Skills Transfer Protocol

### 1. Pre-Match Routine (12 minutes)
**Minutes 0-5: Headshot Warmup**
- 50 static headshots (precision focus)
- 50 moving targets (tracking)
- Focus: "Line of the head" crosshair placement

**Minutes 5-9: 1v1 Duel Simulation**
- Practice stop-shots: A/D → full stop → headshot
- Treat A+D spam as failure
- One correction maximum per target

**Minutes 9-12: Map-Specific Prefires**
- Practice 5 common head-level angles
- Rehearse utility + isolation setups

### 2. MM Mindset Conversion
**"Make MM Like Duels" Protocol:**
- **Isolation Priority:** Use flashes/smokes to create 1v1 scenarios
- **Stop-Shot Discipline:** Treat movement spam as automatic failure
- **Head-Level Anchoring:** Crosshair "under the chin" default position
- **One-Correction Rule:** Maximum one micro-adjustment per engagement

### 3. Utility Integration for Isolation
**Flash + Prefire Combos:**
- Flash → peek with crosshair pre-placed at head level
- Practice 3 head-level prefire spots per map
- Smoke one-ways to force predictable enemy positions

## Long-Term Atomoxetine Protocol

### Course Structure (6-8 weeks)
**Weeks 1-2: Introduction Phase**
- Atomoxetine: Start 18mg daily (morning)
- Remove L-Tyrosine and DLPA completely
- Reduce caffeine to 100mg max per session
- Maintain Alpha-GPC and L-Theanine

**Weeks 3-4: Optimization Phase**
- Atomoxetine: 25-40mg daily (adjust based on response)
- Caffeine: Can increase back to 135mg if well-tolerated
- L-Theanine: May need reduction to 150-175mg
- Monitor for overstimulation

**Weeks 5-8: Stabilization Phase**
- Maintain effective atomoxetine dose
- Fine-tune caffeine/theanine ratio
- Gradual reintroduction of occasional L-Tyrosine (training only)

**Stack Compatibility During Course:**
- ✅ Keep: Alpha-GPC, L-Theanine, Noopept, Caffeine (reduced)
- ❌ Remove: L-Tyrosine, DLPA (dopamine competition)
- ⚠️ Monitor: B-vitamins (may enhance atomoxetine effects)

## Performance Metrics & Tracking

### Weekly Measurements
**Aim Training Metrics:**
- HS-DM: Headshot percentage and KPM
- Arena 1v1: Clean headshot ratio
- Flick accuracy: Micro (5-10°) vs Macro (30-90°)

**MM Performance Metrics:**
- Headshot percentage per match
- Stop-shot success rate (A/D → stop → headshot)
- Impulsive movement incidents per round
- 1v1 duel win rate in MM context

**Cognitive Metrics:**
- PVT reaction time consistency
- Go/No-Go false positive rate
- Subjective focus rating (1-10) pre/post match

### 2-Week Adjustment Protocols

**If Experiencing Overarousal/Jitters:**
- Reduce caffeine by 25-50mg
- Increase L-theanine by 50-100mg
- Remove L-tyrosine completely
- Add 5-minute meditation pre-match

**If Experiencing Underarousal/Sluggishness:**
- Maintain caffeine at 135mg
- Reduce L-theanine by 50mg
- Consider L-tyrosine 500mg (training only)
- Increase Alpha-GPC to 450mg

**If Experiencing Headaches/Overstimulation:**
- Reduce Alpha-GPC to 300mg
- Increase water intake
- Consider splitting caffeine dose
- Add magnesium glycinate (evening only)

## Micro-Routines for MM Chaos Management

### The "Head-First" Checklist (2-3 seconds per engagement)
1. **Eyes → Head:** Spot enemy head position first
2. **Crosshair → Head:** Micro-adjust to head level
3. **Stop → Shoot:** Full movement stop before firing
4. **One Correction:** Maximum one micro-adjustment
5. **Spray Control:** Only spray if stationary and close

### Emergency Protocols for High-Pressure Rounds
**When Feeling Rushed/Impulsive:**
- Take one deep breath before each peek
- Verbally say "head level" before engaging
- Force 0.5-second pause after movement stop

**When Aim Feels "Off":**
- Reduce sensitivity by 10% temporarily
- Focus on crosshair placement over flicking
- Switch to tap-firing only for 2-3 rounds
